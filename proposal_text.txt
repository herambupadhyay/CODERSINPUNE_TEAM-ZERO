01 / EXECUTIVE SNAPSHOT
A continuity layer for teams
ProjectPulse preserves not just project files, but the evidence, relationships and permissions needed to continue 
work responsibly.
Project knowledge rarely lives in one place. A plan may describe a task, meeting notes may explain why it changed, 
a technical document may record the dependency, and a person may be the only one who knows what remains 
unfinished. When that person leaves, the organisation can retain the files while losing the context that makes those 
files useful.
ProjectPulse is a universal AI handover assistant designed to reconstruct a project’s current state from authorised 
documents and prepare a role-appropriate handover when responsibilities change. It builds a persistent, structured 
memory of people, tasks, decisions, dependencies, risks and evidence, then uses that memory to create a transition 
package for a specific incoming person.
CORE PRODUCT PROMISE
A successor should be able to understand what is happening, why it is happening, what remains unfinished, what 
could go wrong, and what they are authorised to do next - with important claims traceable to their source.

What makes the proposal different
Departure Impact Analysis: identifies the tasks, decisions, dependencies and unresolved risks affected when a named 
member exits.
Authority Graph: models scoped delegation and access as explicit relationships rather than assuming that seniority or 
team membership grants universal access.
Evidence-first generation: each important statement is tied to a source location and labelled as supported, inferred, 
conflicting or missing.
Human-controlled continuity: a responsible person corrects and approves the handover before controlled sharing.
Round 1 scope decision
The first demonstrable version will be document-first and project-focused. It will support authorised PDF, DOCX, 
TXT, Markdown and CSV inputs, one project workspace, structured extraction, evidence-linked reconstruction, a 
departure-triggered handover, human approval and controlled successor access. Live integrations with chat 
platforms and repositories are intentionally deferred.

Why ordinary documentation and generic summarisation fall short
Fragmentation: each source describes only part of the project and may use different names or dates.
Time: old notes can conflict with newer decisions; a summary must distinguish current state from history.
Missing evidence: a plausible answer is not necessarily a verified organisational fact.
Permissions: access to one connected document must not automatically reveal everything inside it or every related 
source.
No transition lens: a generic summary does not answer what the departing person owns, what becomes ownerless, or 
what the successor should do first.
OUR PROBLEM DEFINITION
Teams need a trusted transition process that reconstructs project state from authorised evidence, surfaces 
uncertainty, identifies the impact of a person’s departure and shares only the information the incoming person is 
allowed to receive.
Scope of the problem we will solve
ProjectPulse will focus on the knowledge-handover moment: a member leaves, changes role or transfers 
responsibility. It will not attempt to replace project-management software, become a general-purpose enterprise 
search engine, or automate managerial decisions. The product’s job is to make a transition understandable, 
actionable and controlled.

04 / PROPOSED SOLUTION
A hybrid project memory + handover assistant
ProjectPulse continuously organises authorised project knowledge, then activates a focused handover workflow 
when a transition occurs.
How the hybrid model works
LAYER
WHEN IT OPERATES
WHAT IT DOES

updated.

The handover is assembled, not merely summarised
For a selected departing member and incoming role, the system retrieves only authorised evidence and assembles a 
continuation view containing: project overview; transferred responsibilities; work in progress; decisions and their 
supporting sources; dependencies and blockers; risks and recurring issues; important contacts where permitted; 
missing or conflicting information; and a prioritised next-step plan.
Distinctive product mechanisms
Departure Impact Analysis: traces the departing member’s known ownership and relationships to show affected tasks, 
dependencies, approvals and knowledge areas.
Knowledge Gap Radar: highlights undocumented procedures, ownerless work, unsupported claims and contradictions 
that need human attention.
Decision Trail: reconstructs decision sequence and links the current state to source evidence. It does not invent 
rationale when the source does not contain it.
Role-adaptive handover: changes the level and scope of detail according to the successor’s approved role and 
permissions.
Authority Graph: models who can grant, view, edit, approve, delegate and share within a defined project scope.
Human review and acknowledgement: enables correction, rejection, approval, version history and receipt 
confirmation.
KEY PRINCIPLE
Receiving someone’s knowledge does not mean inheriting their authority. ProjectPulse transfers the minimum useful 
context for the new responsibility - not the departing person’s permissions.
Product boundaries
The AI recommends and organises; it does not independently certify truth, decide employment matters, grant 
privileges or publish a handover without approval. A user remains responsible for confirming the content and the 
recipient’s access.

Trigger and feedback loop
A handover can be initiated by an authorised owner or reviewer by selecting the departing member, the 
responsibilities being transferred and the incoming role. The departing member may be invited to answer targeted 
questions about unresolved gaps. New information is marked as member-provided, linked to its origin and routed 
through review; it is not silently treated as verified fact.
After the handover, corrections and new project documents update the project memory. A later handover can therefore 
reuse the reviewed context while still checking freshness and permission scope.

Evidence model
Every important knowledge item stores a source identifier and precise location where available: document name,

07 / AUTHORITY GRAPH & DELEGATION
Make access a first-class part of handover
ProjectPulse models authority separately from project knowledge. This prevents a correct handover from becoming 
an accidental disclosure.
Authority is explicit, scoped and delegable
The Project Head or Workspace Owner acts as the root authority for a workspace. They can define roles and grant 
scoped powers to team leads - for example, authority over backend documentation, operations records or research 
material. A lead may further delegate only the specific powers and resources they have been granted, within the 
same or narrower scope.
AUTHORITY DIMENSION
EXAMPLE CONTROL
Resource scope
Project-wide, department, task group, document, knowledge 
category or individual record.
Allowed action
View, upload, edit, assign, approve, delegate or share. These are 
separate privileges.
Delegation limit
A delegate cannot grant broader scope or stronger actions than 
they hold. Delegation may be time-bound and revocable.
Review boundary
Approval and sharing rights are not automatically included in 
ordinary contributor access.
Auditability
Record who granted, changed, delegated, revoked, approved or 
acknowledged an access decision.
Example: new backend successor
The incoming backend developer receives approved API documentation, assigned backend tasks, relevant decision 
history, migration status and the next actions needed to continue the work. They do not automatically receive 
finance, HR, unrelated research files, global workspace administration or the departing member’s approval rights. A 
project lead can grant additional access when there is a justified need.
NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE
A person’s place in an organisational hierarchy does not automatically expose every lower-level resource. Access is 
determined by explicit permissions and scope, not by graph proximity or seniority.
How the two graphs work together
The Knowledge Graph identifies the information relevant to a transition. The Authority Graph and permission 
service determine which of those sources and claims the requester may retrieve, review and share. The graphs are 
connected through resource references, but the Knowledge Graph is never treated as an access-control mechanism.

10 / TRUST, PRIVACY & FEASIBILITY
Permission checks before intelligence
The organiser explicitly requires that connected sources do not become universally visible, and that uncertainty and 
human approval remain visible throughout the workflow.
Security and responsible handling
Authorised-source rule: users may upload only documents they are permitted to share; demonstrations use synthetic, 
public, permissioned or anonymised data.
Least privilege: access is granted for a defined resource and action; default access is restricted until explicitly granted.
Pre-retrieval enforcement: the backend checks the requesting user’s effective permissions before retrieving source 
chunks or sending them to the model.
Output enforcement: generated handover items retain source permissions; content is filtered or redacted before 
display and sharing. The model prompt is not the security boundary.
Human control: an authorised reviewer may edit, reject or approve; no external sharing occurs before approval.
Audit and lifecycle: grants, revocations, delegation, approval, version changes and acknowledgement are logged. 
Revoked access must stop future retrieval and display.
Data minimisation: process only the documents and context needed for the selected project and handover; keep 
secrets out of prompts and logs.

Design principle for scale
AUTOMATE PREPARATION, NOT ACCOUNTABILITY
ProjectPulse should make project context easier to find and safer to transfer. It should never replace the person 
responsible for deciding whether a claim is correct, whether access is appropriate or whether a handover is ready to 
publish.
Alignment to the challenge
The proposal directly addresses the brief’s expected outcome: authorised source ingestion, project-state reconstruction, evidence-
linked summaries, risk and dependency identification, human review, controlled sharing and a clear continuation path for the 
incoming person. The MVP prioritises a functional vertical slice over a broad but shallow feature list.
